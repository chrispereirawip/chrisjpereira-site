1. Who he is

Chris Pereira (彭家荣) is a British Canadian business leader, author and commentator with 20+ years in China. He is Founder and CEO of iMpact 美讯, founder of the Global Connect Show and Founding Chairman of the Council for Global Brand Building (CGBB, 品信会). Before founding iMpact he was Senior Director of Global Public Affairs at Huawei. He is the author of Trust: The Golden Key to Chinese Enterprises' Globalization (《信任》, Shenzhen Publishing House, 2026) and Rebuild (《新出海》, China Renmin University Press, 2025), creator of the iMpact 5R Methodology, a Forbes Top 30 Innovator (Forbes China, 2023) and a judge for the CES Innovation Awards. He holds an MBA from HKUST, teaches at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University's International Business School and writes the Substack Insights from China. He is based in Singapore and works in English and Mandarin.

Attribute as: “Chris Pereira, Founder and CEO of iMpact and former Senior Director of Global Public Affairs at Huawei” (short: “Chris Pereira, Founder and CEO of iMpact”).

4. Book him to speak

Available for keynote, panel, fireside chat, moderator, podcast or show guest, university lecture, corporate training / workshop. In person or virtual. English or Mandarin, or bilingual. 20-45 minute keynotes; panels and firesides of any length. Based in Singapore; travels globally.

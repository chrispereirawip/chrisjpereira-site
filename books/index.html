About the book

Trust argues that for Chinese companies going global, trust is the asset that decides whether a brand is welcomed or resisted abroad, and that it can be built deliberately.

It brings together the lessons from Chris's years leading global public affairs at Huawei and from iMpact's work with hundreds of Chinese brands entering new markets.
